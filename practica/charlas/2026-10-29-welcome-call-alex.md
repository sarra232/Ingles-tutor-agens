## 🏆 Logros Destacados (Brechas Cerradas en Vivo)
1. **Artículo `a` en profesión (G1)**: *"I work as **a** Java developer"*.
2. **Present Perfect + `for` para antigüedad (G2)**: *"I **have worked** in this company **for** more than 5 years"*.
3. **Pregunta de profesión natural (G3)**: *"What do you do?"*.
4. **`near` sin `to` (G4/V5)**: *"I live near Medellín, Colombia, in a little town"*.
5. **Gerundio después de `enjoy` (G6)**: *"I enjoy walking with my dogs"*.
6. **Conector `Regarding` aplicado al instante**: *"Regarding a bug..."*.
7. **Expresión idiomática natural**: *"do you follow me?"* (¿me sigues la idea?).

## 🔧 Correcciones y Expresiones Nuevas Aprendidas

| Lo que dijiste | Versión Natural Corregida | Por qué / Regla Clave |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *this town is cold but I feels so live* | *this town is cold, **but it feels so alive*** | Sujeto **`it`** (el pueblo) + **`alive`** (`/əˈlaɪv/` = vivo / lleno de vida). |
| *I live a rural zone, sorrounded of nature* | *I live **in** a rural **area**, **surrounded by** nature* | **`live in`** + **`surrounded by`** (rodeado por/de). |
| *I really love the nature* | *I really love **nature*** | `nature` en general **nunca** lleva `the`. |
| *help to teammates* | ***help our teammates*** | Igual que `attend`, **`help` NO lleva `to`** antes de la persona. |
| *take it and try fixing as soon as possible* | ***we** take it and try **to fix it** as soon as possible* | Nunca omitir el sujeto (**`we`**) ni el objeto (**`it`**) después de `fix`. |
| *meanwhile my hobbies* | ***As for my hobbies** / **Regarding my hobbies*** | `Meanwhile` = mientras tanto. Para cambiar de tema usa **`As for...`** o **`Regarding...`**. |
| *I'm very active person* | *I'm **a** very active person* | Siempre usar **`a`** antes de `adjective + singular noun` (`person`). |
| *do you preffer stay at home or leave* | *do you **prefer staying** at home or **going out**?* | "Salir a pasear/divertirse" = **`go out`** (`leave` es marcharse de un lugar). |
| *in diferents projects* | *in **different** projects* | Los adjetivos en inglés **nunca** llevan `-s` plural. |
| *we can (cobrar) extra hours* | *we can **charge overtime** / **get paid overtime*** | "Horas extras" = **`overtime`** (`/ˈoʊ.vɚ.taɪm/`). |
| *if the bug in the low enviroments... at the first hour* | *if the bug **is** in the **lower environments**... **first thing in the morning*** | No olvidar el verbo **`is`**; ambientes bajos = **`lower environments`**; a primera hora = **`first thing in the morning`**. |

## 📋 Tu Discurso Completo del Roleplay (Versión Nativa Pulida para Leer en Voz Alta)

> **Turno 1:**
> *"Hi Alex, nice to meet you too! Welcome to the team, it's a pleasure to work with you. I live near Medellín, Colombia, in a little town. I work as a Java developer and I'm 32 years old. I really love nature—I live in a rural area, surrounded by nature. Its landscapes are green and wonderful; this town is cold, **but it feels so alive**! What do you do, how old are you, and do you have children?"*
>
> **Turno 2:**
> *"Well, a normal day on the team is to attend different meetings, do our job, and always pay special attention to the dashboards to monitor the application for issues, errors, and downtime. If something extraordinary occurs, **we** take it and try **to fix it** as soon as possible, or **help our teammates** in case someone needs help or asks something. **As for my hobbies**, I really enjoy nature and walking with my dogs, or something simple like staying at home taking care of my pets and plants, and cleaning the house. When I want to relax, I watch anime, cook something, play video games, visit my mother and nephews, or go to the gym and play tennis. I'm **a** very active person! What about you? Do you prefer staying at home or **going out**? Do you practice any sport?"*
>
> **Turno 3:**
> *"Oh, nice! You are very active too. What is your favorite soccer team? Do you follow any? I **have worked** in this company **for** more than five years, always as **a** Java developer, but in **different** projects. **Regarding** the bug, well, everything depends. If the bug **is in production** and the company **loses** a lot of money or users **get angry**, unfortunately we can't wait until tomorrow—we fix it today and **charge overtime**. But if the bug **is** in the **lower environments** and it doesn't have **a** big impact, **we** can work on that tomorrow **first thing in the morning**, do you follow me?"*
